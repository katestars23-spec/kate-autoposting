Kate Autoposting

Kate Autoposting is a private tool used only by Kateryna Starovitskaya to upload her own videos to her own YouTube channel.

The tool uploads finished videos as private (not public). Publishing, scheduling and deleting videos are done manually by the channel owner in YouTube Studio.

The tool is not offered to other people and does not access anyone else's data.

The tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service.
