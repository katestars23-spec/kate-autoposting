Privacy Policy — Kate Autoposting

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Who we are

Kate Autoposting is a private tool operated by Kateryna Starovitskaya (contact: businessbloggerstar@gmail.com). It is used only by its owner to upload videos to her own YouTube channel.

What Google data the tool uses

The tool requests one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload (upload videos to YouTube). It uses it only to upload the owner's videos, with their title, description and thumbnail, as private videos on the owner's channel, and to read back the ID and channel of the uploaded video to confirm it went to the correct channel.

The tool does not read, change or delete other videos, comments, subscribers, analytics or any other account data.

Storage

The access token is stored only on the owner's computer, in the macOS Keychain. Video files and their descriptions are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is stored on any server operated by us.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. The only recipient of data is YouTube/Google itself, as part of the upload. A short notice with the link to the uploaded video is sent to the owner's own Telegram account.

Other users

The tool is not offered to the public and does not collect data from anyone other than its owner.

YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that the stored token stops working and is deleted from the Keychain. To request deletion of any data, write to businessbloggerstar@gmail.com.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be published on this page.
